About

Neon Drop is a free Android arcade game. You fall through a neon tunnel that never ends, threading the gaps between gates while the course bends, intruders arrive, and something starts following you down. One thumb, portrait, no timer and no finish line — only how far you get.

The descent

  • Gates drift, then close, then split into two ways through
  • The course starts to bend, and intruders show up in it
  • Five kinds of alien — Drifter, Chaser, Sentry, Swarm, Titan — each with its own habits
  • Something follows you, and from a certain depth there is no bottom left

Gear

  • Ten equippable items: slow, wide, magnet, shield, steady, fortune, double score, blaster, freeze, ghost
  • Item slots you can widen with the Item Storage upgrade
  • Eight passive upgrades — coin and score bonuses, an easier start, a wider PERFECT window, pull, guard, auto-turret, luck
  • Thirty skins, all earned with coins

Free to play

No purchases. Everything in the game is earned by playing. The game is supported by ads.
