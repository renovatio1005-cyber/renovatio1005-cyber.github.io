About

Lumio is a free Android wallpaper app. Every wallpaper in the library is 4K resolution or higher, so images stay sharp on modern high-density displays instead of being upscaled from smaller sources.

Features

  • A curated library filtered to 4K and above
  • Browse by category, colour, or search
  • Swipe left and right to move through wallpapers full-screen
  • Crop and position before applying to home or lock screen
  • Save favourites for later
  • Light and dark themes
